Each part's `tools:` in `.apptown/app.yaml` determine its checks. Every package manifest (package.json, go.mod, pyproject.toml, requirements.txt, Gemfile, Cargo.toml, pom.xml, a .csproj, composer.json, project.godot) must be under a part naming its tool or a `notAppCode:` folder (documentation, tooling). Ignore node_modules, vendor and testdata. For findings, add the tool to its part or the folder to `notAppCode:`.
