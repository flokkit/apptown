We had a supplier recall last spring and it took Fenella two days of phone calls
to find which clinics had the affected batches. I want a recall button.

When a supplier sends a recall notice, someone in central office enters the
product and one or more batch numbers, the date of the notice and the
supplier's reference. Then:

- every clinic holding any of those batches sees it at the top of their page,
  with how many doses they have and which fridge;
- that stock is held straight away so nobody can record it as used;
- each clinic confirms when they have physically pulled it out and how many
  doses they actually found (it won't always match);
- we need a list of animals vaccinated from those batches in the last
  12 months, if the batch was recorded at the time of use, so the vets can
  decide whether to contact owners. Where the batch wasn't recorded, say so;
  don't leave it off.

I want to see one page showing each clinic: not yet confirmed, confirmed with
count, or "doesn't hold this batch". Batch numbers sometimes get typed with or
without dashes and in lower case; matching shouldn't miss them because of that.
