The regulator has given us a date: the inspection is on Tuesday 17 November.
From now on I want a rule for the drugs register:

- For the two weeks before any announced inspection, no changes go live on the
  register, full stop. This time that's from Tuesday 3 November.
- The only exception is fixing something that is actually wrong with the
  record (a wrong balance, a lost witness). Those still need my sign-off, and
  also Adaeze's, and the reason has to be written down with the change.
- New features can be prepared during the freeze, just not put live.

I'll tell you the dates of each inspection as soon as I get them. If I'm away,
Adaeze can sign off in my place, but not for a fix she reported herself.
