Now that everything is in the tracker I want it to tell me what to chase,
instead of me working it out every morning. These are the rules I use in my head.

## When something is overdue

Each step has a time limit, counted from when the referral reached its current
status:

| Status | Urgent | Soon | Routine |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sent, waiting for the specialist to accept | 4 working hours | 2 working days | 5 working days |
| Accepted, waiting for an appointment date | 1 working day | 5 working days | 15 working days |
| Seen, waiting for the report | 2 working days | 5 working days | 10 working days |

Working hours are 8:00–18:00 Monday to Friday. Weekends and public holidays
don't count. I'll give you the public holiday list each year; until it's there,
treat every weekday as a working day.

"Appointment booked" isn't chased by me until the appointment date has passed.
If the appointment date is past and nobody has marked it "seen" by the end of
the next working day, it's overdue for me to check whether they went.

## My chasing list

One page, overdue first (most overdue at the top), then due today, then the
rest. Urgent ones always above soon, soon above routine, within each group. Each
row shows how long it's overdue in plain words ("2 working days overdue"), not
a timestamp.

When I chase, I record that I did (phone or email, and a note). Chasing doesn't
change the status, but it resets the clock for that row to one more period of
the same length, so it drops down the list until it's due again. The original
overdue time should still be visible in the history.

## Worked examples

- Urgent referral from Dunmore Quay sent Friday 16:30. Four working hours:
  1.5 hours on Friday, then 2.5 on Monday, so it's due Monday 10:30.
- Routine referral sent Wednesday 10:00, accepted the next Tuesday. The
  "accepted" clock starts Tuesday; the earlier wait doesn't carry over.
- Soon referral, seen on Monday 3rd, report not back. Due Monday 10th at the
  same time. I phone on Tuesday 11th; next due Tuesday 18th.
- A referral changed from routine to urgent by the vet halfway through: use the
  urgent limit from the time of the change, not from the start.

## Edge cases

- **The pet has died.** The vet marks the referral closed with reason
  "deceased". It must drop off my chasing list straight away and never send
  anything to anyone. Please make sure the wording on screen is gentle, just
  "Closed – patient deceased".
- **Owner declines** after we've sent it (cost, usually). Closed, reason
  "owner declined". Keep it for the history.
- **Specialist declines** (wrong service, full). That's not closed: it goes back
  to the referring vet to choose another centre, and it should keep the history
  of the first attempt. Show it on my list as needing the vet.
- **Re-referral**: the same pet goes back to the same specialist months later.
  New referral, but link it to the old one so the vet can see it.
- Times are our local time. Mind the clock change in spring and autumn; a
  4-hour urgent limit across the changeover is still 4 real working hours.
