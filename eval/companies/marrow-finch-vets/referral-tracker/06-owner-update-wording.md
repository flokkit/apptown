The text we copy into the owner email when a referral is accepted says "your
pet has been accepted for treatment". Owners read that as "it's going to be
fixed". Can it say "the specialist team has agreed to see [pet name] and will
contact you to arrange an appointment" instead? Adaeze has okayed the wording.
