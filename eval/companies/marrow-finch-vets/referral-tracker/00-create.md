I chase every specialist referral for all 14 clinics and right now it lives in
my inbox and a whiteboard. I'd like a simple web app for it.

A referral is: the pet (name, species, breed, the owner's surname and our
patient number), which of our clinics and which vet is referring, which
specialist centre it's going to and which service (orthopaedics, cardiology,
oncology, dermatology, ophthalmology, neurology, imaging, other), how urgent it
is (routine, soon, urgent), and a short clinical reason.

Then I need to see where each one is: sent, accepted by the specialist,
appointment booked (with the date), seen, report back, closed. Or cancelled.

There are about 20 specialist centres we use regularly. I want to keep a list
of them with phone and email so people pick from the list instead of typing.

The vets want to see their own referrals; clinic managers their clinic's; I
need to see everything, sorted so the ones that need chasing come first. We
don't need owners to use it, it's for us.
