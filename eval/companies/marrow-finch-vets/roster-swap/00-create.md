Can we have a little web app where vets and nurses can put up a shift they
can't do and someone else can take it? Right now it's a WhatsApp group and
swaps get lost. The clinic manager should be able to see what's been swapped,
and the shift should show against the right person afterwards. Shifts belong
to a clinic and have a start and end time and a role (vet, nurse, student nurse).
