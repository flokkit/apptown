About Fenella's approval rule: it's been three weeks and managers are approving
50 swaps a week without looking, and swaps sit unapproved overnight so nobody
knows who's coming in. Fenella and I have talked and we've agreed to change it.

Only swaps that involve a Friday, Saturday or Sunday night shift need the
manager's approval. Everything else counts straight away as before. The fatigue
rules still apply to everything, approved or not. If a weekend-night swap isn't
approved within 24 hours, it goes to me.
