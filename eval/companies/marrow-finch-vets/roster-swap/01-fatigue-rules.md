The swap app is being used a lot, which is good, but I'm seeing people pick up
shifts they shouldn't. Last month a nurse at Carrow Bridge did a night, then a
day, then another night because of three swaps, and she fell asleep driving
home. Nobody broke a rule on purpose; nobody could see the whole picture. I want
the app to stop unsafe swaps before they happen. Fenella and Adaeze agree.

## Rules for taking a shift

Someone can only accept a shift if, **after** the swap, their own shifts still
meet all of these:

1. **Rest**: at least 11 hours between the end of one shift and the start of
   the next.
2. **Length**: no more than 13 hours in any single stretch of work. Two shifts
   that touch (one ends 19:00, the next starts 19:00) count as one stretch.
3. **Nights**: no more than 4 night shifts in a row. A night shift is any shift
   with at least 3 hours between 23:00 and 06:00.
4. **Weekly hours**: no more than 48 hours in any 7-day window (rolling, not
   Monday to Sunday).
5. **Role**: a vet shift can only be taken by a vet. A nurse shift by a
   registered nurse. A student nurse shift by a student or registered nurse.
   Surgery-day nurse shifts (marked on the shift) only by nurses with the
   anaesthesia monitoring sign-off.
6. **Clinic**: anyone can take a shift at any clinic, but if they haven't worked
   at that clinic in the last 6 months, say so and make them tick that they
   know where things are. Don't block it.

If a rule would be broken, don't let them accept, and tell them which rule in
plain words: "You'd only have 8 hours off between your Tuesday night and this
Wednesday shift. You need at least 11."

On-call from home doesn't count as a shift for these rules unless they're
called in; we'll deal with that later.

## Worked examples

- Ana works Mon 08:00–18:00. A shift Tue 06:00–14:00 is offered. 12 hours
  rest. OK.
- Same Ana, shift Mon 22:00–Tue 07:00 offered. Only 4 hours rest after
  Monday's day. Refused (rule 1).
- Ben works Wed 07:00–19:00. Offered Wed 19:00–21:00. Touches, so one stretch of
  14 hours. Refused (rule 2).
- Carla has nights Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu (all 20:00–08:00). Offered Fri 20:00–08:00.
  Fifth night in a row. Refused (rule 3). If she's offered Sat night instead,
  there's a full night off in between, so the run of nights resets and the rest
  is fine (Fri 08:00 to Sat 20:00 is 36 hours). But it's still refused, on
  rule 4: five 12-hour nights inside 7 days is 60 hours. The message should
  name the hours rule, not the nights rule.
- A shift 18:00–01:00 has 2 hours between 23:00 and 06:00. Not a night shift.
  A shift 18:00–02:00 has 3 hours. Night shift.

## Edge cases

- The person **giving away** the shift: check nothing for them; giving away
  only makes their week safer.
- A shift that's been offered and accepted is swapped. If the rules change
  later, don't undo past swaps, but show managers a list of people currently
  rostered in breach so they can fix them.
- Clock changes: a night shift across the spring change is an hour shorter in
  real time. Use real hours.
- Two people try to accept the same shift at the same moment. Only one gets it;
  the other sees "someone took this a moment ago".
