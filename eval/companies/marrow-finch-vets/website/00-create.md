Our website is an old template nobody can edit, and the clinic hours on it
are wrong in at least three places. I'd like a new, plain site. It doesn't have
to be fancy; it has to be right and quick to load on a phone.

Pages:

- **Home**: who we are in two sentences, a big "Emergency? Call" box with the
  after-hours numbers, and links to find a clinic.
- **Clinics**: all 14, each with address, phone, opening hours, and a
  "Directions" link that opens the phone's map app. A list, sorted by name,
  with a simple map at the top. Each clinic has its own page too.
- **Emergencies and after hours**: Carrow Bridge Animal Hospital is open
  24 hours, (01632) 960 400. Northgate is open 19:00–08:00 weekdays and all
  weekend, (01632) 960 455. Which one to call depends on the clinic, so the
  page should list each clinic and where its after-hours calls go. Adaeze will
  give us a short "is this an emergency?" list (breathing trouble, collapse,
  suspected poisoning, etc.).
- **Services**: vaccinations, neutering, dentistry, surgery, nurse clinics,
  referrals. A paragraph each; I'll send text.
- **Careers**: a short intro and a link to our jobs board. We don't take
  applications on the site.
- **Register as a new client**: name, email, phone, pet's name and species,
  preferred clinic, and a message. It goes to the chosen clinic's reception
  email. Nothing else; we don't need addresses or dates of birth. Keep the
  submissions for 12 months, then delete them.

The clinic hours and phone numbers must come from one place so they can't
disagree between pages. Nothing goes live without me seeing it, and the
emergency wording needs Adaeze.
