# Marrow & Finch Veterinary Group

An invented group of 14 small-animal veterinary clinics across a mix of market
towns and one city, about 230 staff: 70 vets, 110 nurses and student nurses,
the rest reception and support. Two of the clinics run after-hours emergency
services (Carrow Bridge Animal Hospital, open 24 hours, and Northgate, open
nights and weekends). A small central office handles HR, pharmacy compliance,
marketing and IT. They are opening a fifteenth clinic, Saltmarsh Road, in
February 2027.

The clinics: Ashcombe, Brindle Street, Carrow Bridge Animal Hospital, Dunmore
Quay, Eastholt, Fernlea, Gallow Hill, Harlow Marsh, Inchbrook, Kestrel Park,
Lindenford, Millbeck, Northgate, Wrenfield.

Their software is a handful of small internal apps that replaced paper books
and shared spreadsheets, plus the public website and the occasional campaign
site. One person in IT looks after all of it.

## How we work (house rules)

1. **The Controlled Drugs Register is a legal record.** Any change to it needs
   Ravi's sign-off before it goes live. Every such change must come with a test
   showing that existing entries cannot be edited or deleted, only corrected by
   a new entry that points at the one it corrects.
2. **Public pages need marketing approval.** Nothing on the website or a
   campaign site goes live without Joaquín's approval.
3. **Clinical wording is reviewed by a vet.** Anything public or owner-facing
   that describes symptoms, treatments, medicines or vaccines is reviewed by
   Adaeze or a vet she names, in addition to marketing.
4. **One list of clinics.** Fenella owns the clinics and their hours. When a
   clinic opens, closes or changes hours, every app that lists clinics changes
   in the same go, and the website's emergency numbers are checked by a second
   person before they go live.
5. **Rosters are about safety, not just convenience.** Changes to the swap
   rules (rest periods, who may cover what) need Mele's approval.
6. **No production changes on Fridays or the day before a public holiday**,
   unless it is fixing something broken. Tomasz decides what counts as broken.
7. **Owner details are kept to what we need.** Forms collect the minimum and
   new-client enquiries are deleted after 12 months.
