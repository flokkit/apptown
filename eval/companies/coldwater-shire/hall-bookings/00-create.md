We look after six community halls: Coldwater Memorial Hall, the Coldwater
Senior Citizens Room, Harrow Flat Hall, Tumby Creek Recreation Reserve
Pavilion, Ellisford Hall and Marrowbank Hall (closed for renovation until
next year). Bookings are a paper diary at the front counter and a lot of
phone tag.

I'd like:

- A page on the website where anyone can see which halls are free on
  which days (just "booked" or "available", no names) and send a booking
  request: which hall, date, start and finish time, what it's for, how
  many people, their name and contact details, and whether they're a
  community group.
- My team sees requests come in, checks them, and confirms or declines.
  The person gets an email either way.
- Each hall has its own hourly rate and capacity. Keep that somewhere we
  can change without ringing IT.
- When a booking is confirmed, show what they owe. The bond and how the
  money works I'll send separately because it's complicated.
- Key pickup: we need to know who has the key for each booking.
