Here's how the money works for hall bookings, as adopted in the fees and
charges and the Community Facilities Hire Policy. Please make the system
work it out so my team stops doing it on paper.

**Hire charges**

| Hall                          | Per hour | Full day (over 6 h) | Bond  | Capacity |
|-------------------------------|----------|---------------------|-------|----------|
| Coldwater Memorial Hall       | $42      | $230                | $500  | 220      |
| Coldwater Senior Citizens Rm  | $22      | $120                | $200  | 40       |
| Harrow Flat Hall              | $30      | $160                | $300  | 120      |
| Tumby Creek Pavilion          | $25      | $140                | $300  | 80       |
| Ellisford Hall                | $30      | $160                | $300  | 100      |

- Charged per started hour, minimum 2 hours. 2 h 10 min is 3 hours.
- If the hourly total would be more than the full-day rate, charge the
  full-day rate. Full day means one calendar day, not 24 hours.
- Bookings that run past midnight are charged as one booking, but the
  full-day cap applies per calendar day.
- Setting up and packing up count as hire time.

**Who pays what**

- **Registered community groups** (on the council's community group
  register) pay 50% of the hire charge. The bond is the same.
- **Regular bookings** (same group, same hall, at least 10 times in the
  financial year) get a further 20% off the hire charge after the
  community group discount. Only bookings confirmed and held count
  towards the 10; cancelled ones don't.
- **Council's own use** and **polling places** pay nothing and have no
  bond.
- **Events with alcohol** pay double the bond, whoever they are.
- **Private parties for 18th and 21st birthdays** pay a $1,000 bond
  regardless of hall. Councillors complained about Harrow Flat after
  last year's.

**Cancellations**

- Cancelled 14 days or more before the booking: full refund of hire and
  bond.
- 7 to 13 days before: 50% of the hire charge kept, bond refunded.
- Less than 7 days: hire charge kept, bond refunded.
- If council cancels (storm damage, a hall needed as an evacuation
  centre, an election), everything is refunded, whatever the date.
- Days are counted from the date we receive the cancellation to the date
  of the booking, so a cancellation on Monday 1st for Monday 15th is 14
  days and gets the full refund.

**Bond refunds**

- After the booking, the hall caretaker does an inspection and records
  "OK" or "issues" with notes and photos.
- If OK, the bond is refunded in full. If issues, the facilities officer
  decides how much to keep, with a reason. Whoever decides how much to
  keep can't also be the person who approves the refund going out. We had
  an audit finding on that.
- The refund has to be approved within 21 days of the booking. Show us
  anything older than 14 days that hasn't been approved.
- The amount kept can never be more than the bond. Anything over that is
  invoiced separately, outside this system.

**Worked examples**

1. Netball club (registered community group), Tumby Creek Pavilion,
   Saturday 9:00–15:30 (6.5 hours, so 7 started hours). 7 × $25 = $175,
   capped at the $140 full-day rate, then 50% off: $70. Bond $300.
2. Same club's 11th confirmed booking this year, 2 hours: 2 × $25 = $50,
   50% off = $25, then 20% off = $20. Bond $300.
3. Private 21st, Coldwater Memorial Hall, 18:00–00:30 with alcohol.
   Started hours are counted from the booking start, so that's 7 hours:
   six on Saturday and one on Sunday. Saturday is 6 × $42 = $252, capped
   at $230. Sunday is 1 × $42 = $42 (the 2-hour minimum is for the whole
   booking, not each day). Total $272. Bond is the bigger of $1,000 (21st)
   and double the hall bond ($1,000), so $1,000, not $2,000.
4. Book club, Senior Citizens Room, cancels 10 days before: 50% of hire
   kept, bond back.
5. Ellisford Hall booked for a wedding reception, council takes the hall
   as an evacuation centre during the fires: everything refunded.

Where a booking matches more than one bond rule, the highest single bond
applies. Bonds never add together.
