Our website is on a hosted CMS that we're leaving when the contract ends
in March. We want our own, simpler site that the comms team can update.

Pages we need from day one:

- Home: the next bin day lookup, the latest three news items, and links
  to the most-used services (pay rates, report a pothole, book a hall,
  register your dog, planning notices, meeting papers).
- Services: about 30 pages, one per service. Plain text and links,
  mostly. Sione will move the content across.
- Bin day lookup: type your street, pick the town if the street name
  exists in more than one, and see your general waste, recycling and
  green waste days for the next four weeks. The collection calendar
  comes from the waste contractor as a spreadsheet each year: streets
  grouped into zones A to F, and which zones go on which day. Public
  holidays shift collection to the next day.
- Contact: phone, email, counter hours, postal address, and the after-hours
  number for emergencies (storms, livestock on roads, water main breaks).
- News: Lena and Sione write items with a date, a title, a photo and the
  text.
- Accessibility statement.

Sione and I need to be able to add and edit pages and news ourselves. It
should load fast on a phone in a dead spot out past Marrowbank.
