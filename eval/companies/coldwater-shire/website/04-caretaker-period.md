The caretaker period for the local government elections runs from
15 September to 24 October (election day). During that time, the
website must not:

- show photos, quotes or profiles of any councillor, including on the
  "Your councillors" page. That page should just list ward names and say
  councillor information is unavailable during the election period;
- publish news that mentions a councillor or candidate by name.

Any new news item or new page during caretaker needs the CEO's office
(Bronwyn) to approve it before it goes live. Fixing a typo on an
existing service page doesn't.

On 25 October everything goes back to normal, but the councillor pages
will need the new council's details, so don't just switch the old ones
back on.
