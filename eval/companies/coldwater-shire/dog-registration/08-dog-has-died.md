We had an owner ring up in tears because she got an overdue reminder for
a dog that died in June. She'd told the counter but nobody could mark it
anywhere.

Please add a way to record that a dog has died, moved out of the shire,
or been surrendered, with the date and who recorded it. From that date:
no more reminders, it drops off the overdue list, and the tag number is
retired (never reissued). The dog's record stays, we need the history.

If someone records it by mistake, a senior ranger can undo it with a
note. And if the dog died before the overdue run but we only hear after,
don't send anything else, not even "thank you for letting us know".
