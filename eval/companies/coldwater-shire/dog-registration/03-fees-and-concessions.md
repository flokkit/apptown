Council adopts the fees each June in the budget. Right now staff look them
up in the fees schedule PDF and do the sums on a calculator. I want the
system to work out what's owed. These are the 2026–27 fees:

| Category                                   | Fee  |
|--------------------------------------------|------|
| Desexed dog                                | $45  |
| Not desexed                                | $150 |
| Working dog (farm, on rural land)          | $25  |
| Registered breeder's dog (not desexed)     | $60  |
| Declared dangerous or menacing dog         | $310 |
| Assistance dog                             | $0   |

**Concessions**

- Pension or concession card holders get 50% off. This applies to the
  desexed, not-desexed and working-dog fees only, never to dangerous or
  menacing dogs.
- Dogs over 10 years old get 50% off too, but you can't stack it with
  the pension concession. Use whichever is better (they're both 50%, so
  just don't make it 75%).
- A dog under 6 months old on 1 July pays nothing that year but still
  needs a tag.

**Pro rata**

- A dog registered for the first time after 1 January pays half the fee
  for that year. "First time" means first time with us: a dog that was
  registered last year and wasn't renewed until February is late, not new,
  and pays the full fee.
- Pro rata comes off after the concession, so a pensioner's new desexed
  dog in March pays $45 × 50% × 50% = $11.25. Round to the nearest
  5 cents, so $11.25.

**Unknown desexed status**

- If we don't know whether the dog is desexed (blank on the import), do
  not work out a fee. Show "need desexing status" and make the staff
  member ask.

**Overrides**

- A senior ranger can override the fee with a reason (for example a
  hardship waiver approved by the manager). The calculated fee and the
  override both stay on the record. Customer service can't override.

**Worked examples**

1. Desexed dog, owner with a pension card, renewed in July: $22.50.
2. Not desexed, 11 years old, no concession card: $75.
3. Declared menacing dog, owner with a pension card: $310.
4. Working dog on a rural property in Tumby Creek, first time with us,
   registered in April: $12.50.
5. Pup born 1 March, registered 15 April: $0, tag issued.
6. Dog registered last year, renewed 3 February: full fee, plus it shows
   as late on the overdue report for this year.

Next June the fees change. I'd like to enter next year's table myself
without a software change, and last year's fees must not change for
registrations already paid.
