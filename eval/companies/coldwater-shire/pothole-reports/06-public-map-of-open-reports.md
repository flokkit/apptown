Following on from the councillor question, the CEO's office agreed that
instead of a councillor-only screen we'd put a public map on the website
showing reported potholes and their status. Everyone sees the same thing,
councillors included.

Please show: the location, the date reported, the priority once assessed,
and the status (reported / scheduled / fixed in the last 30 days). Leave
out "not ours" reports and anything older than that.

Do not show photos (people's cars, kids, houses end up in them), names,
contact details, or the description text, because residents write all
sorts in there. The map has to work with a screen reader too, so a plain
list view underneath with the same information.
