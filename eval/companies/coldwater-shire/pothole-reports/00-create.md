We need a proper way for people to report potholes. Right now they ring
Deepa's team, someone writes it on a form, it gets emailed to me, and half
the time the crew turns up and can't find it.

What I want:

- A public form on the website. Where is it (drop a pin on a map, or type
  the street and nearest cross street), a photo if they've got one, a
  short description, and their name and phone or email in case we need to
  ask. Name and contact are optional but encouraged.
- They get a reference number when they submit.
- On our side, a map of all open reports with a list next to it. Click one
  and see the photo, the description, when it came in.
- Crew leaders can mark a report as "scheduled", "fixed" or "not ours"
  (state highway, private road, that kind of thing) with a note. Fixed
  means a date and who fixed it.
- I want to filter by town: Coldwater, Harrow Flat, Tumby Creek, Ellisford,
  Marrowbank, and "rural".

Only depot staff and customer service should get into the staff side.
Customer service need to be able to enter a report on someone's behalf when
they ring in.
