The Chamber of Commerce wants us to publish the feedback we've had, so
people can see we're listening. Fine, but carefully:

- A "What you've told us" page with each month's feedback grouped by
  theme (parking, noise, access to shops, trees, other), and a count for
  each, with a few example comments.
- Example comments are chosen by my team, with any names, phone numbers,
  shop names that identify a person, and house numbers taken out. We
  approve each one before it goes up.
- The full feedback stays with us. It's never on the site.
- Add a line to the form saying what we will and won't publish.
