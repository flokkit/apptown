The Main Street, Coldwater upgrade starts in February and runs about 14
months: new footpaths, kerbs, trees, lighting and a raised crossing near
the school. Businesses on Main Street are nervous and we want one place
for everything.

The site needs:

- An overview: what's being done and why, a map of the four stages
  (Stage 1: Railway Pde to Hume St, Stage 2: Hume St to Bank St, Stage 3:
  Bank St to the roundabout, Stage 4: side streets).
- A "this week" page: which sections are closed, which detours, parking
  changes, and the next two weeks so businesses can plan deliveries.
  Updated every Friday by Sione.
- A timeline showing each stage, planned and actual.
- An FAQ.
- A feedback form: name, email (optional), which business or street if
  relevant, and the comment. Comes to Lena's team.
- A sign-up for the weekly update email.

It's separate from the main website but should look like council. It'll
be taken down after the project finishes.
