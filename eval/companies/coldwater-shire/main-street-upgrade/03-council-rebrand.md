Council adopted the new brand at the September meeting. New logo, new
colours, new typeface. It launches on Monday 2 February, and everything
residents can see has to switch over the same day: the website, Planning
Notices, the hall booking pages, and the Main Street upgrade site. Not
one on Monday and the rest whenever. The Mayor is doing a launch at the
library at 10 am.

The brand guide is on the shared drive (Communications > Brand 2026). The
short version:

- Primary colour is "Reservoir" #1F4E5F, accent "Wattle" #E2A72E, text
  #1B1B1B on white.
- The Wattle accent must never be used for text on white, it fails
  contrast. It's for lines and shapes only.
- Logo files: the full logo for headers, the square mark for small spaces
  and browser tabs. Alt text is "Coldwater Shire Council".
- Typeface is Source Sans 3 for everything.
- The old green (#3A7D44) and the old tree logo must be gone everywhere,
  including emails the apps send and any PDFs they produce.

I'd like to see all four on a test address the week before so the CEO's
office can look before launch.
