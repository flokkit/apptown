Development applications that are open for public comment have to be
published. At the moment the planning team emails a Word document to
comms, Sione pastes it into the website, and twice last year a notice
went up a day late, which means the comment period has to be restarted.

I'd like a simple public site, just pages, nothing to log into, that
lists every application currently open for comment. For each one:

- the application number (DA-2026-0143 style)
- the property address and the town
- what's proposed, in a sentence
- the applicant: company name, or "Private applicant" for individuals
- the date comments open and the date they close
- the plans as PDFs
- how to make a submission (email or post, the address and reference)

The planning team keeps the list in a spreadsheet already. The site
should be rebuilt from that data, not edited by hand. Once the closing
date has passed, the application drops off the current list and moves to
a "recently closed" page for 60 days.

These are statutory notices. The exact text that went up and the date it
went up need to be provable later, because objectors sometimes argue they
weren't given the full period.
