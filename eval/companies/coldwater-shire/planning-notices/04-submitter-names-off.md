We've had a complaint. The "recently closed" page for the Tumby Creek
quarry expansion included the planning team's summary of submissions,
and the summary named three residents who objected and the street they
live on. One of them has since had someone turn up at their door.

From now on:

- No names, addresses, phone numbers or emails of submitters on any page
  of this site, ever. Summaries refer to "a submitter" or "12 submitters".
- Applicants who are individuals stay "Private applicant", and their
  property address shows only because the law requires it.
- Before anything is published, it should be checked for names and
  contact details of people, and stopped if it finds any. I'd rather
  hold up a notice for a day than do this again.
- Take the three names down now, and check every page that's ever been
  published for the same problem. Tell me what you found.

We still need to keep a record of what was published and when, for the
statutory period. But that record is not public.
