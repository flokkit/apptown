Following the audit committee's recommendation, planning notices are
official records of council and need my sign-off before they're
published. That applies to new notices and to any change to a published
notice, even fixing a typo, because a change can affect the comment
period.

How I'd like it to work:

- When the planning team updates the data, I see what would change on
  the site: which notices are new, which are changed and what changed.
- I approve or send it back with a comment.
- Only the version I approved goes live. If someone changes the data
  after I approve, it needs approving again.
- When I'm on leave, Bronwyn Halloran signs off as my delegate. Nobody
  else, and the planning team can never approve their own notices.
- Keep my approval with the published version, so we can show the audit
  committee who approved what and when.

Closing dates passing and notices moving to "recently closed" doesn't
need my approval. That's just the calendar.
