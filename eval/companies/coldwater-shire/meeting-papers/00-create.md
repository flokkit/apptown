Council meeting papers are on a shared drive and on the website as PDFs
that Sione uploads by hand. Searching for an old resolution means asking
me, and I have to open twenty PDFs.

I need a proper home for them:

- Meetings: ordinary council meetings (monthly), special meetings, and
  the audit and risk committee. Each meeting has a date, a type, and a
  place (council chambers, or one of the halls when we meet in the towns).
- For each meeting: the agenda, the attachments, the minutes, and later
  the confirmed minutes. All PDFs, some of them large (the budget
  attachment is 80 MB).
- The files go in proper storage, not the database and not someone's
  laptop.
- A public page listing meetings with their papers, newest first, and a
  search across the text of agendas and minutes.
- Some agenda items are confidential (contracts, staff matters, legal
  advice). Those attachments must never be public. Councillors and
  governance staff can see them.
- Papers have to go up at least 3 working days before the meeting.
  Show me any meeting that's inside that window with no agenda.

Nothing published here is ever deleted. If a document has to be replaced
(wrong attachment), the original stays in the archive marked as
superseded, with who replaced it and why.
