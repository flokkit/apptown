A news story last month quoted our draft minutes as if they were final,
and one of the resolutions was corrected at the next meeting. So:

- Minutes go up as "Unconfirmed" with that word on every page of the
  public view, not just the title.
- They only become "Confirmed" when the next ordinary meeting resolves to
  confirm them. Whoever marks them confirmed records the resolution
  number from that meeting.
- If council confirms them with changes, the corrected version is
  uploaded and the unconfirmed one is kept as superseded.
- I sign off each set of minutes before they go public, unconfirmed or
  confirmed. My delegate is Bronwyn Halloran when I'm away.
