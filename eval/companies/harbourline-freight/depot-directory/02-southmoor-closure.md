Southmoor depot closes on Friday 27 November and its work moves to Merrin
Junction. In the directory:

- Up to and including 27 November, Southmoor's page stays as is, with a notice
  at the top: "Southmoor closes on Friday 27 November. From Monday 30 November
  use Merrin Junction."
- From 28 November, Southmoor comes off the list. Its page stays reachable (old
  links are in emails) but says it's closed and points to Merrin Junction.
- Merrin Junction's page shows 8 bays from 30 November (bays 7 and 8 are new,
  not refrigerated), and Southmoor's after-hours number moves to Merrin
  Junction's on-call roster — same number, it's a mobile.
