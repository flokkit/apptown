The board has approved a tighter night-work rule from 1 January. Our policy
rule 6 becomes:

- No more than **3** shifts in any 7 days that include work between 00:00 and
  05:59 (was 4).
- And after any such night shift, at least 10 continuous hours of rest before
  the next shift (new).

Windows that are entirely before 1 January are judged under the old rule. A
7-day window that crosses 1 January is judged under the new rule, but only
shifts on or after 1 January count towards the 3. The monthly report should
show which rule version each breach was judged under.

Example: Jo does night shifts on 29, 30 and 31 December and 1 and 2 January.
Under the new rule, only 1 and 2 January count, so no breach on 2 January. If
Jo does one more on 3 January, that's still only 3 — no breach. A fourth on
4 January breaches.

This goes through the two-sign-off rule.
