After the split shift problem I want a firmer rule for this app.

From now on, any change to how hours are totalled or how breaches are worked
out needs two approvals before it goes live: mine (or Anika's when I'm away),
and one depot manager, because they're the ones who'll notice if it's wrong on
the ground. The same person can't be both.

And every such change has to show it still gets the right answers on the worked
examples from the original brief, plus Raj's split shift. I want to see those
results when I approve, not just be told the tests passed. If we add an example
later, it joins the list.

Changes to colours, wording and screens that don't touch the calculation only
need me, as before.
