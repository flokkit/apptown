The gate office spends half the morning on the phone taking bookings. I'd like
our regular carriers (about 25 companies) to book their own slots.

- Each carrier gets its own login. They can only see and change their own
  bookings, never anyone else's, and they shouldn't see who else is booked —
  just which times are free.
- Same rules as when the gate books it.
- They can cancel up to 2 hours before the slot. After that they have to ring us.
- Every booking they make should say it was made by the carrier, so I know
  who to blame.
- Gate office can still book, move and cancel anything.

Start with three carriers as a trial: Coolstore Logistics, Banks Bros Haulage and
Westline Couriers. I'll send the contact emails.
