Our current website was built by an agency in 2019 and we can't change anything
on it without a quote. I want a new one we can update ourselves.

Pages:

- **Home**: who we are in two sentences, the three services, a photo of the
  fleet (I'll supply), and a contact button.
- **Services**: linehaul between our depots, metro delivery, refrigerated
  freight, warehousing and cross-dock at Port Kellan. A paragraph each.
- **Depots**: the five depots with address, office hours and phone. Not the
  after-hours numbers, those are internal.
- **Careers**: a short paragraph and a link to current roles. (The driver
  recruitment site will link from here later.)
- **News**: dated posts, newest first. I'll write them.
- **Contact**: a form (name, company, email, phone, what it's about: quote /
  existing delivery / claims / something else, message). Quote enquiries go to
  sales@harbourline.example, claims go to Mele, everything else to
  reception@harbourline.example.

It must be fast on a phone and not look like a template. Our colours are navy
(#14315A) and signal orange (#F26A1B). No cookie tracking beyond what we
actually need, and a privacy page that says what the contact form keeps and for
how long (90 days, then deleted).

Nothing goes live without my approval, including later changes.
