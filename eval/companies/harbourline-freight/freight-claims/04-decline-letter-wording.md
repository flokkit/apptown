The letter the app makes when we decline a claim is too blunt. One customer
rang Dana about it. Please change it to:

> Dear {name},
>
> Thank you for letting us know about consignment {consignment}. We've looked
> carefully at the photos and the delivery records, and we're sorry to say we
> can't accept this claim, because {reason}.
>
> If you have more information — for example photos taken at delivery or a
> signed docket noting damage — please reply to this email and I'll look at it
> again.
>
> Kind regards,
> {my name}
> Claims, Harbourline Freight

And the reasons in the drop-down should read like the end of that sentence
("the goods were signed for in good condition", not "SIGNED_CLEAN").
