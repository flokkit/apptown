Hi all,

We need 40 new drivers by February — mostly linehaul, some metro — and the job
boards aren't getting us enough people. Siobhan and I want a small site just for
the recruitment drive that we can put on the trucks, on radio ads and on social
media. Working name: "Drive with Harbourline".

**Pages**

1. **Home**: one line ("Good runs, home most nights, paid properly"), a photo of
   a real driver (Mick Tane has agreed), the three things drivers ask about —
   pay, routes, time at home — and a big "Register your interest" button.
2. **Pay and conditions**: for each type of role, the hourly rate, the
   overtime rate, allowances, and the roster pattern. Lucia will supply the
   figures. Must say clearly these are the rates under our current enterprise
   agreement and when they were last updated.
3. **Routes**: the regular runs from each depot, with typical start time and
   whether you're home that night. A simple map would be nice but a list is
   fine.
4. **FAQs**: about 12 questions; I'll send the list.
5. **Register your interest**: the form.

**The form**

- Name, mobile, email, suburb.
- Licence class (rigid, heavy combination, multi-combination) and years
  holding it.
- Which depot(s) they'd like to work from.
- Linehaul, metro, or either.
- When they could start.
- "Anything else we should know?" (free text, 1,000 characters max).
- A tick box agreeing we can contact them about driving jobs, and a link to a
  privacy statement.

Don't ask for date of birth, licence number or anything about health — we do
that later, properly, at interview. Compliance (Tomasz) was clear about that.

Submissions go to a list only the recruitment team (me, Kiri and Jordan) can
see, and I'd like an email to recruitment@harbourline.example for each one.
We need to be able to export the list to a spreadsheet.

**Keeping data**

People who don't get hired: we keep their details for 6 months after the drive
closes, then delete them. The privacy statement has to say that.

**Dates**

Launch 2 November. The drive closes 30 November. After that the form comes down
and the site says thanks, the drive is closed, and links to the Careers page.

Siobhan approves everything public, and because the form collects personal
details Tomasz wants to see it before it goes live too.

Thanks,
Lucia
