Three more questions for the FAQ, these keep coming up on the phone:

**Do I need my own truck?**
No. All our drivers drive Harbourline trucks. We don't use owner-drivers on
these roles.

**I've got a rigid licence. Can I still apply?**
Yes. We have metro rigid roles, and we'll support good drivers to upgrade to a
heavy combination licence after 12 months with us.

**Will I be on nights?**
Some linehaul runs leave late in the evening. Metro roles are day shifts. The
Routes page shows the start time and whether you're home that night for every
regular run.
