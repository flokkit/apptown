Our current website was built by an agency in 2019 and every change costs us
a quote and three weeks. We'd like to replace it with a plain, fast site we
can change ourselves. Pages:

- Home: who we are, the main products, a link to internet banking (the
  existing address, we're not changing that), and a short line on member
  ownership.
- Everyday accounts, savings and term deposits, personal loans, home loans:
  one page each with what the product is, who it's for, fees, and the
  current rates.
- Rates: all current rates on one page.
- Branches: the six branches with address, opening hours and a map link.
  ATMs too.
- Financial hardship: how to ask for help (Fiona will give us the text).
- Complaints: how to make a complaint and how to go to the external
  complaints scheme (Fiona's text).
- Contact us.
- Footer on every page: our licence and registration numbers, the
  disclaimers, privacy policy, terms of use.

Rates must come from the Rate Sheet's data (Arjun knows), never typed in.
Every disclaimer, comparison rate warning and target market statement is
Fiona's text, word for word; I'll place them but I'm not allowed to change
them.

It must work well on a phone (most of our visitors are on one), be readable
for older members (bigger text, good contrast), and work without JavaScript
for the important bits.
