Here's the text for the financial hardship page. Please use it exactly. The
headings can be styled however Kwame likes.

> **Having trouble making repayments?**
>
> If you're finding it hard to keep up with a loan or credit card
> repayment, talk to us as early as you can. You don't have to be behind on
> your repayments to ask for help.
>
> **How to ask**
>
> Call us on 1300 000 412 (Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 5:30pm), email
> hardship@bramblestreetcu.example, or visit any branch. Tell us which
> loan, what has changed, and what you think you can afford.
>
> **What happens next**
>
> We'll confirm we've received your request and may ask for some
> information. We'll give you a decision within 21 days. If we can't help
> in the way you asked, we'll tell you why in writing.
>
> **If you're experiencing family or domestic violence**
>
> You can ask to speak to one of our trained staff, and you don't need to
> give us documents before we can help. If you're in danger, call 000.
>
> **Free financial counselling**
>
> Free, independent financial counsellors can help you work through your
> options. Call the free financial counselling line on 1800 000 007.
>
> **Not happy with our decision?**
>
> You can make a complaint to us, and if you're still not satisfied, to
> the external complaints scheme. See our Complaints page.

Please also put a "Having trouble with repayments?" link on every loan page.
