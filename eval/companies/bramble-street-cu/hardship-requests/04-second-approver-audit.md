Internal audit reviewed 40 hardship files from the last half-year. Two
findings affect the app:

1. In three files the "risk approval" was recorded by a team leader in
   member services who had been given risk access temporarily while I was
   on leave and it was never taken away. From now on, only people in the
   risk team can give the second approval, and acting arrangements have an
   end date that the app enforces. If there's no end date, there's no acting
   arrangement.
2. In one file the assessor changed the arrangement length from 3 to 6
   months after I had approved 3. The app allowed it. Any change to an
   arrangement after approval must go back for approval (I believe this was
   in Tomasz's original rules, but it isn't working for the length field).

Separately, audit asked how we control changes to the app itself. Please make
sure any change to Hardship Requests needs my approval or my delegate's
before it goes live, not just the developer's and Tomasz's. I'll want to see
the list of changes made since the app started at the next audit committee.
