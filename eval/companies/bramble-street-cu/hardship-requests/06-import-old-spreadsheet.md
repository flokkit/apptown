We still have about 260 open and recently closed requests in the old
spreadsheet ("Hardship Register 2024-26.xlsx" on the member services
drive, Hyun-woo can get you a copy). Please bring them into the app so we
have one place. Some things to know about the sheet:

- There's one tab per year, and 2025 has a second tab called "2025 (2)"
  that is mostly duplicates of the first. Match on member number and
  received date. If they differ, the "(2)" tab is newer.
- Dates are a mix: most are day/month/year, but anything entered by the
  Millbrook branch in early 2025 is month/day/year. If a date can be read
  both ways and it's from Millbrook, assume month/day.
- The "Outcome" column is free text. "Approved", "appr.", "OK 3 mths",
  "yes" all mean approved. "NFA" means withdrawn. Anything else, leave the
  decision empty and put the text in a note.
- Some rows have the member's name but no member number. Don't guess. List
  those for us and we'll fix them by hand.
- A few rows have notes like "DV - see Fiona". Those are family violence
  requests and must get the restricted visibility from the assessment rules.
- Closed requests older than 2 years can come in as closed with whatever
  history we have. Don't make up history entries for them; mark them as
  imported.

Please don't paste any rows into tickets or chat when asking us questions.
Use the row number and tab name.
