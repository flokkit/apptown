One of my team noticed that when a member's two loans are reduced to a total
of $150, the letter says the new repayments are $75.00 and $74.99. The member
rang to ask where the other cent went. I tried it with $100 across three
loans and got $33.33 three times, so $99.99. Small, but members do notice.
