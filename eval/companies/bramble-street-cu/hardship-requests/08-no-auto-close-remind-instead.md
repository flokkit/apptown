Sorry, I need to undo the auto-close from last week. Fiona pointed out that
the hardship rules say we must try to contact the member at least twice
before treating a request as withdrawn, and a closure the member never heard
about would look terrible if it came back as a complaint.

So instead:

- Nothing closes on its own.
- At 14 days with no response, the request shows "second contact due" and
  goes to the top of the assessor's list.
- The assessor records the second contact attempt (how and when).
- Only after 10 more days with no response can they close it as "withdrawn
  - no response", and they have to confirm the two contact dates.

The six requests that were auto-closed last week need to be reopened, with
a note on each saying why. Don't delete the closure, just add the reopening
after it so the history is honest.
