Hi team,

We need to get hardship requests out of the shared mailbox and the big
spreadsheet. At the moment a member rings or emails, someone in the contact
centre types it into the sheet, and then it sits in the hardship inbox until
someone picks it up. We lost track of two last quarter and one turned into a
complaint.

What I want is a simple internal web app for my team:

- Log a new request: which member (member number and name), which loan or
  loans it's about, how they contacted us, the date we received it, what
  they're asking for (pause repayments, reduce repayments, extend the term,
  something else), and the reason in their words.
- A list of open requests showing how long each one has been open, who's
  assessing it and its status (new, waiting on member, being assessed,
  decided, closed).
- An assessor can pick one up, add notes, record documents we've asked for
  and received, and record a decision: approved (with what we've agreed and
  for how long), declined (with the reason), or withdrawn.
- Every change is kept with who did it and when. We have to be able to show
  the regulator the whole history of a request.

Only member services and risk staff should see it. Hyun-woo can tell you how
staff sign in.

The actual assessment rules I'll send separately, they need some thought.
