Staff currently find our rates in a PDF I make in Excel after each ALCO
meeting. Branches print it and pin it up, and then the old one stays pinned
up next to the new one. We've had members quoted last month's rate twice
this year.

I'd like a simple internal Rate Sheet: one page staff open in the browser,
showing current rates for:

- savings accounts (base rate and bonus rate, with the bonus conditions in
  a short line)
- term deposits by term (1, 3, 6, 9, 12, 24 months) and by amount tier
  ($5,000 to $49,999, $50,000 and over)
- personal loans (secured car, unsecured), with comparison rates
- home loans (variable owner-occupier, variable investor, 1, 2 and 3 year
  fixed), with comparison rates

Each rate shows the date it took effect. The page shows "Rates as at" with
the date and time it was published.

The rates should live in one data file that I update, and the website will
need to read the same data later, so please keep it tidy and not tangled up
with the page.

A new rate sheet can only go up when both I and Fiona have approved it
(house rule 3). Nothing goes up on my say alone.
