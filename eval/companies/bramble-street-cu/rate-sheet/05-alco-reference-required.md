exercises: a way-of-working change; the approval of rate changes must be bound to evidence (the ALCO minute) rather than to an approver's say-so
---

The external auditor tested our rate controls this month. They picked five
rate changes and asked us to show each one's ALCO approval. We found four.
The fifth, a term deposit special in August, was approved by Arjun and me
by email, but ALCO never formally approved it. We both thought the other
had checked.

From now on:

- A rate change can't be approved for publishing unless it names the ALCO
  meeting and item, and attaches the signed minute extract.
- When I approve, I should see the minute next to the rates it covers, so
  I'm approving the match, not just the numbers.
- A "special" (a rate for a limited time) also needs an end date, and the
  sheet must go back to the normal rate on that date by itself.

This is a change to how we work, not only to the Rate Sheet. The website and
the campaign site read these rates and should follow the same rule.
