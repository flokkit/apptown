We're launching a First Home Loan offer on 2 November, running until
31 January 2027. I need a small campaign site at its own address
(firsthome.bramblestreetcu.example) with:

- A headline and short explanation of the offer: a discounted variable rate
  for first home buyers, no application fee, borrow up to 95% of the
  property value with no lenders mortgage insurance for members who have
  saved with us for 12 months.
- A repayment calculator (Arjun will send the rules).
- An eligibility checklist people can tick through: first home, buying to
  live in, at least 18, borrowing at least $200,000, member for 12 months
  for the no-LMI part.
- An enquiry form: name, phone, email, preferred branch, best time to call,
  and a tick box to agree to be contacted. Enquiries go to the lending team
  inbox.
- The terms, the target market statement, and the comparison rate warning,
  all from Fiona.

The rate comes from the Rate Sheet like everything else. After 31 January,
the site must stop taking enquiries and say the offer has closed.

Enquiries are people's personal details, so the same rules as member data:
not in logs, not kept longer than we need (Fiona says 12 months unless they
become a loan application).
