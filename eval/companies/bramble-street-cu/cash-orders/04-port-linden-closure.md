As announced to staff yesterday, the board has decided to close the Port
Linden branch. Its last day of trading is Friday 27 November 2026. Members
will be served by Harrow Vale, and we're keeping an ATM at Port Linden.

For Branch Cash Orders this means:

- No cash deliveries to Port Linden after Thursday 26 November. Port
  Linden's last order is a return, not a delivery: everything it holds
  comes back to the vault on 26 November.
- Harrow Vale's overnight limit goes up from $150,000 to $200,000 from
  Monday 30 November (the insurer has agreed).
- Port Linden must stay in the history and the reports. We need to be able
  to look up its old orders for seven years.

The same closure has to show up in the Complaints Register and on the
website. Those requests are going in separately, but please treat them as
one change and don't let any one of them go live before the others are
ready. I'd rather all three change on the same day.
