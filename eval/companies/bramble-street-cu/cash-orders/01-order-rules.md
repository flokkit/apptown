These are the cash order rules. Fiona has seen them and is fine with them.

## Holding limits

Each branch has an insurance limit on the cash it can hold overnight. An order
must not take a branch above its limit, based on the branch's closing cash
figure for the previous business day (branches type that in each morning
for now).

| Branch | Overnight limit |
|--------|-----------------|
| Bramble Street | $400,000 |
| Eastgate | $250,000 |
| Millbrook | $180,000 |
| Harrow Vale | $150,000 |
| Ashcombe | $150,000 |
| Port Linden | $120,000 |

Projected holding = yesterday's closing cash + order total - expected
withdrawals for the delivery day (a figure the branch types in, can be zero).

If the projected holding is above the limit, the branch can still submit
but it needs my approval and Fiona's, and the order says why.

## Order sizes

- Minimum order $10,000. Below that, wait and combine.
- Notes in bundles of 100 notes only: a $50 bundle is $5,000. If someone
  types $7,500 in fifties, tell them it has to be a multiple of $5,000.
- Coins in standard bags: $2 bag $50, $1 bag $50, 50c bag $20, 20c bag $20,
  10c bag $10, 5c bag $5. Orders in multiples of a bag.
- Any single order over $100,000 needs Fiona as well as me, regardless of the
  holding limit.

## Cut-off and delivery days

- The armoured car comes Tuesday and Thursday only.
- Orders must be fully approved at the branch by 2pm on the second
  business day before delivery. Normally that's Friday 2pm for a Tuesday
  delivery and Tuesday 2pm for a Thursday.
- Public holidays aren't business days, so they push the cut-off earlier.
  If the delivery day itself is a holiday, there's no delivery that day.
  Don't move it automatically, the branch picks another date.
- Times are head office local time.

## Worked examples

1. Eastgate closed yesterday with $180,000, orders $60,000 for Thursday,
   expects $30,000 withdrawals. Projected = 180 + 60 - 30 = $210,000. Under
   $250,000. Normal approvals.
2. Port Linden closed with $95,000, orders $40,000, expects $5,000 out.
   Projected = $130,000, over $120,000. Can submit with a reason; needs me
   and Fiona.
3. Millbrook wants $120,000 for a Tuesday. Over $100,000, so needs Fiona
   even though Millbrook's holding would be fine.
4. Ashcombe submits at 2:05pm Friday for Tuesday. Too late. The app offers
   Thursday instead.
5. Christmas: Friday 25 December and Monday 28 December (Boxing Day,
   moved) are both holidays on our calendar. The second business day before
   Tuesday 29 December is Wednesday 23 December, so the cut-off is 2pm that
   day.

## Returns

Returns to the vault follow the same cut-offs. No limit check (they bring the
holding down). Returns over $100,000 still need Fiona.
