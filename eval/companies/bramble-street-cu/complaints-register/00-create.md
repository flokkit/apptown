We are required to keep a register of every complaint, record how and when
we responded, and report numbers to the regulator every quarter. Today
that's a spreadsheet Rosa maintains and a lot of calendar reminders.

Please build a Complaints Register for staff:

- Anyone on staff can log a complaint: who it's from (member or not; member
  number if they are one), how it came in (phone, email, branch, letter,
  social media, via the external complaints scheme), the date and time
  received, which branch or team it's about, the product, a category, and
  what the person said.
- Rosa's team (complaints) assigns an owner, records the acknowledgement,
  any requests for more information, and the final response with the
  outcome (upheld, partly upheld, not upheld, withdrawn) and any remedy
  (refund amount, fee reversal, apology, other).
- The register shows each complaint's deadlines and whether it's on track.
  I'll send the deadline rules separately because they're fiddly.
- Nothing is ever deleted or overwritten. Corrections are new entries.
- Everyone can log, but only the complaints team and risk can see the full
  register. A staff member can see the complaints they logged.

Categories to start: fees and charges, interest rates, service, lending
decision, hardship, account access, privacy, other.
