Every quarter we send the regulator a complaints return. Can the register
produce it? The numbers they want, for the quarter:

- complaints received, by category and by how they came in
- complaints closed, by outcome
- how many final responses were sent on time, late with a delay notice, and
  late without one
- median days to close (calendar days, not counting stopped days)
- total refunds and fee reversals in dollars
- complaints still open at quarter end, and how many are past deadline

A complaint received in one quarter and closed in the next counts as
received in the first and closed in the second. Early resolutions count as
closed. Withdrawn counts as closed with outcome withdrawn. Fiona signs the
return, so I need to be able to download it, check it, and then mark it as
submitted with the date, after which that quarter's figures are frozen.
