Now that the board works, I want it to suggest the cover so I'm only checking,
not building it from nothing. Here's how I decide. Please make the suggestions
follow these rules, and let me change any suggestion before I publish it.

**Who can cover**

- Our own teachers with a free period that period, who are at the right campus.
  Travel between campuses takes a period, so a teacher teaching at Ridgeview in
  period 2 can't cover at Harbour Road in period 3.
- Casual relief teachers who've said they're available that day.
- Not anyone who is themselves on the absence list, obviously, and not the
  Principal, the deputies, or the heads of school.

**Limits**

- Our own teachers do at most 2 relief periods in a day and at most 5 in a
  fortnight (the two-week timetable cycle). This is in the enterprise agreement,
  so it's a hard limit, not a preference.
- No teacher should lose all their free periods in a day. If they only have one
  free, they can't be given it.
- Casual relief teachers can do up to 6 periods a day (a full day); if we've
  called them in, we pay them for the day anyway, so use them first.

**Who is best**

In order:
1. A casual relief teacher already booked for the day.
2. A teacher who normally teaches that class.
3. A teacher of the same subject area.
4. Whoever has done the fewest relief periods this fortnight.
5. If it's still a tie, whoever has done the fewest this term.

Science practical lessons (labs) need a science teacher, or the lesson changes to
"theory only" and the board says so. Senior PE practical needs someone with a
current first aid certificate; the casual list includes that.

**Worked examples**

1. Ms Adeyemi-Clarke (Maths) is sick all day. Mr Lindqvist, casual, is booked
   and available. He gets all four of her classes; nobody else is used.
2. Two absences in period 3, one Year 9 Science lab and one Year 7 English. Free
   that period: Dr Osei (Science, 1 relief this fortnight) and Ms Barker (History,
   0 this fortnight). Dr Osei takes the lab, Ms Barker takes English, even though
   Ms Barker has done fewer — the lab needs the science teacher.
3. Mr Haddad already has 2 relief periods today. He's the only teacher free in
   period 6. He can't be given it; the class shows as uncovered and I'll deal with
   it (usually by combining classes or taking it myself).

**Edge cases**

- If an absence is cancelled after I've published the board (they turn up after
  all), the relief teacher should be told they're released, and that period
  doesn't count towards their limit.
- If a relief period is given and then the teacher's own class is cancelled
  (excursion), that doesn't free them from relief they've already been given.
- Combining two small classes in one room is something I do by hand. The board
  should let me mark it, and it counts as one relief period for whoever has it.
- The counts reset at the start of each fortnight, but I want to see the whole
  term so I can tell if someone's being leaned on.
