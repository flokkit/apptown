Every morning between 6:30 and 7:45 I sort out relief cover with a phone, a
spreadsheet and a whiteboard. I'd like one screen that does it.

- Teachers report an absence: who, which day or days, whole day or which
  periods, and a reason (sick, PD, excursion, personal leave). They can do it from
  their phone the night before or that morning.
- The board shows, for today, every class that has lost its teacher, by period.
- I assign a relief teacher to each one. Relief teachers are either our own
  staff with a free period, or casual relief teachers we call in.
- The board shows which classes are still uncovered, in red, so I can see what's
  left at a glance.
- Teachers can leave work for the class (a note, maybe a file).

We have a six-period day at the senior school. The timetable comes out of our
timetabling software as a spreadsheet each term; I can give you that.
