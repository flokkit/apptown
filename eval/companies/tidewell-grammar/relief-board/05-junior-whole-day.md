The junior school doesn't work in periods the way the senior school does. A K–6
class has one teacher all day, and if she's away the class needs one relief
teacher for the whole day, not six different people. Specialist lessons (music,
PE, languages) happen with the specialist and the class teacher has that time
free, but the relief teacher stays with the class.

Can junior absences be covered as a whole day by one person, and only fall back
to splitting it if Callum (or I) decide to? The 2-a-day limit doesn't make sense
for us either — a junior teacher would never be asked to cover a whole other
class during her own teaching day. Our relief is almost always casuals.
