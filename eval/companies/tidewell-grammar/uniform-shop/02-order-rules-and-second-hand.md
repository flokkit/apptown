Here's how ordering actually works, and the second-hand rail, which the P&F
(parents' association) runs with us. Please treat the price and account parts
as needing my approval.

**Who can order**

Parents of current students, and parents of students who have accepted a place
for next year. New families can order from the day their acceptance is recorded
by enrolments; they will not have a fees account yet, so they pay at the counter.

**Paying**

- Charge to fees account: only for families whose account is in good standing.
  The finance system gives us a nightly file with a yes/no per family; we never
  show the parent why it's no, we just don't offer the option.
- Pay at the counter on pickup.
- Families on the uniform assistance programme (about 40 families, confidential)
  get their items at no charge up to an annual amount I set ($350 per child this
  year). Lorraine knows who they are; the volunteers must not. On a packing slip
  it should look exactly like any other order charged to the account.

**Exchanges**

- New items can be exchanged for a different size within 30 days if unworn with
  tags. Lorraine records it; stock goes back up for the returned size.
- No exchanges on socks, hats or anything second-hand.

**Second-hand rail**

- Families donate or consign used uniform. Consigned items are sold at a price
  the P&F sets (usually 40% of new), and the family gets 60% of the sale as a
  credit on their fees account at the end of each term. Donated items: the P&F
  keeps all of it.
- Second-hand items are one-off: each has its own record (item, size, condition
  A/B/C, price, consigned by whom or donated).
- Second-hand items show online only to families who are signed in, not on any
  public page, and are first-come, first-served.

**Worked examples**

1. The Chen family (good standing) orders a junior winter blazer, size 8, at $145,
   charged to account. Lorraine packs it Tuesday, stock goes from 6 to 5, account
   charged $145.
2. The Okonkwo family is on the assistance programme with $350 left. They order
   two summer dresses ($68 each) and a blazer ($145): $281, all at no charge,
   $69 remaining. Packing slip says "Charged to account".
3. Same family later orders a library bag and a hat ($22 + $25 = $47) — still inside
   the $69, no charge. Another $40 order after that: $22 at no charge, $18 charged
   to the account (or paid at counter if they're not in good standing).
4. A consigned senior blazer sells for $58. At the end of term the consigning
   family gets $34.80 credited.

**Edge cases**

- If a consigned item is lost or damaged in the shop, the family still gets their
  60% of the price it was marked at.
- If a child leaves the school, unused assistance doesn't carry over.
- The assistance list is updated by me only. If a family comes off it mid-year,
  items already ordered stay free.
