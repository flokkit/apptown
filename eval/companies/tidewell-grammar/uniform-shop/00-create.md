The uniform shop is open Tuesday and Thursday mornings and is run by Lorraine,
one of our office staff, with parent volunteers. Stock is on a spreadsheet that's
always wrong by the time anyone looks at it, and orders come in by email and on
scraps of paper.

I'd like:

- Stock: each item (e.g. "Junior summer dress"), each size, how many on the shelf,
  the price, and a reorder level.
- Parents can place an order online for pickup on a shop day, or for delivery to
  the child's classroom (junior school only — the senior kids can collect it
  themselves).
- Lorraine sees the orders for the next shop day, packs them, and marks them
  collected or delivered. Stock goes down when an order is packed, not when it's
  placed.
- Payment for now is at the counter or charged to the school fees account. No
  card payments online yet; I'll ask about that later.
- A monthly figure for me: what we sold, what it cost us, what's on the shelf.

Prices are set by me. Nobody else changes them.
