After the athletics photos business (a student whose parents had said no to
photos ended up in a news post), Rachel and I have agreed two things for every
public site the school has — this website, the open day site and the sports
results page, and anything public we make later:

1. Nothing goes live on a public site until I've approved it. That includes small
   text fixes. If I wrote it, someone else in marketing approves it — I'll be
   back to having one assistant next term — or Callum.
2. Before anything goes live, there should be a check for students' full names
   and photos, and anything that has one must have the student's media-consent
   flag recorded as yes. If the check can't tell, it should say so and it doesn't
   go live until a person has looked.

If I've approved something and it's later found to break the consent rule, I'd
like to be able to see what I was shown when I approved it.
