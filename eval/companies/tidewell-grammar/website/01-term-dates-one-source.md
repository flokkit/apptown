We've had three different sets of Term 2 dates this year: the website said one
thing, the library said another, and the relief board had last year's. Parents
noticed.

I'd like the term dates kept in one place, by me, and everything else to read
them from there:

- The website shows them (as it does now).
- The library uses them for due dates and the "no notices in the holidays" rule.
- The relief board uses them for the fortnight cycle and term weeks.
- The excursion consents app should warn a teacher who sets up a trip in the
  holidays, or on a pupil-free day.

For each term: start date, end date, and any pupil-free days inside it. Next year's
dates are usually confirmed by the board in August, so there's a period where
next year's dates are "proposed". Apps shouldn't use proposed dates for anything
that matters (like a due date) until I mark them confirmed, but the website can
show them marked "proposed".

If an app can't get the dates for some reason, it should say so rather than carry
on with old ones.

I've added a short note to each of the other apps too.
