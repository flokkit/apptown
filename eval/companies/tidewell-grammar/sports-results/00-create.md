I'd like a simple public page for inter-house sport. At the moment I write the
results on a whiteboard outside the PE office and email a photo of it to the
newsletter. Parents keep asking where they can see it.

- One page per carnival (swimming in Term 1, cross country in Term 2, athletics in
  Term 3) with the results of each event: event, age group, placings and house.
- A running points table for the year, house by house, with the house colours.
- An archive of past years.

It doesn't need logins or anything clever. I'm happy to fill in a spreadsheet
after each carnival and have that turn into the page. It needs to look fine on a
phone, because that's what parents use at the carnival itself.
