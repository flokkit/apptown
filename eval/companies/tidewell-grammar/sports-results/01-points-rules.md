Here's how points work. The heads of house argue about this every year, so it
needs to be exactly right and the page should be able to show how a total was
reached.

**Placings**

| Place | Individual event | Relay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | 8 | 16 |
| 2nd | 6 | 12 |
| 3rd | 4 | 8 |
| 4th | 3 | 6 |
| 5th | 2 | 4 |
| 6th | 1 | 2 |

**Participation:** every student who competes in an event earns 1 point for their
house, once per event (not per heat). A student who's entered but doesn't start
earns nothing. This is the bit that matters most to me — it's how a house wins
by getting everyone in the pool, not just its stars.

**Ties:** if two students tie, they share the points for the places they cover.
Two tie for 2nd: (6 + 4) / 2 = 5 each, and the next student is 4th. Points can be
halves; show them as 5.5, not rounded.

**Disqualification:** a disqualified student or relay team gets no placing points
but keeps the participation point. Everyone below moves up.

**Divisions:** junior school (ages 8–11) and senior school (12–18) have their own
tables at each carnival, and then a combined school table that's simply the two
added together. Age group is age on 31 December of the carnival year.

**Records:** if a school record is broken, the page shows it with a star. I'll
give you the record list.

**Worked examples** (swimming, 12 years boys 50 m freestyle, eight swimmers):

- Placings: 1st Ridge, 2nd and 3rd tie Cedar and Wattle, 4th Harbour, 5th Ridge,
  6th Cedar, 7th Harbour, 8th disqualified Wattle.
- Placing points: Ridge 8 + 2 = 10; Cedar 5 + 1 = 6; Wattle 5; Harbour 3.
- Participation: Ridge 2, Cedar 2, Wattle 2, Harbour 2.
- Event total: Ridge 12, Cedar 8, Wattle 7, Harbour 5.

Relay, 4×50 m, 13 years girls: Harbour 1st (16), Cedar 2nd (12), Wattle
disqualified, Ridge 3rd (8). Participation is 4 per team that started, including
Wattle. Totals: Harbour 20, Cedar 16, Ridge 12, Wattle 4.

**Edge cases**

- An event with fewer than six starters only awards the places that exist.
- If I correct a result after the page is up (it happens — a timing error), the
  table should recalculate and the page should say "corrected" with the date on
  that event.
- Students can be in only one house. If the spreadsheet has the same student in
  two houses, stop and tell me.

Names on the page: see the school rule about consent. The spreadsheet has a
consent column.
