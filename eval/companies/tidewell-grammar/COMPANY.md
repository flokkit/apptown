# Tidewell Grammar School

An invented independent K–12 day school of about 1,150 students and 140 staff
on two campuses: the junior school (Kindergarten to Year 6) at Ridgeview, and the
senior school (Years 7–12) at Harbour Road, ten minutes apart. Four terms a year.
Every student belongs to one of four houses: Cedar (green), Harbour (navy),
Ridge (red) and Wattle (gold). There is no in-house developer; the IT
coordinator looks after accounts and devices and is the school's contact with
the software team.

## How we work

These are the house rules the school wants the software to keep, whoever is
asking.

1. **No student on a public page without consent.** A student's full name or
   photograph may appear on a public page (the website, the open day site,
   sports results) only when the student's media-consent flag is set. Without it,
   use first name and last initial, or house and year level, and no photo.
2. **Medical information needs the child safety officer.** Any app that stores
   or shows medical notes, allergies or medication, and any change to how that
   information is stored, shown or shared, needs Rachel Kowalczyk's sign-off
   before it goes live. Medical notes are visible only to staff who need them
   for a specific activity.
3. **Public pages are approved by marketing.** Nothing goes onto a public site
   without Hana Kobayashi's approval, including small text edits and new news
   posts. The person who wrote the change cannot also approve it.
4. **Term dates come from one place.** Callum Fitzgerald maintains the term
   dates. Every app that needs them reads that one source; nobody types them in
   twice.
5. **Money goes through the business office.** Prices, charges and payment
   methods are approved by Graham Whitlock.
6. **Junior school parents hear from the school, not the student.** Notices
   about Kindergarten to Year 6 students go to parents, never to the student's
   own address.
