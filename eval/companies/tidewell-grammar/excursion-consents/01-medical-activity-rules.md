Following on from Anjali's request, these are the rules about what a medical
note means for what a child may do on a trip. Right now teachers work this out
in their heads on the day, and they don't always get it the same way. I want the
app to work it out, show it, and stop a trip being marked ready when something is
unresolved.

**Activities.** When setting up a trip, the teacher ticks which activities it
includes. The list is: walking (under 2 km), walking (2 km or more), swimming or
water activity, bush or outdoor setting more than 30 minutes from a hospital,
food provided by us or the venue, animal contact, overnight stay, physical sport.

**Conditions.** Parents pick from a list and can add free text. The list is:
anaphylaxis (and to what), asthma, diabetes (type 1), epilepsy, a heart
condition, a mobility limitation, "other — please describe".

**What each one means:**

| Condition | Activity | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Anaphylaxis | any | Needs an action plan uploaded and an in-date adrenaline auto-injector confirmed by the parent. Without both: **not cleared**. |
| Anaphylaxis to the food allergen | food provided | **Cleared with conditions**: the teacher must tick that the venue has been told. |
| Anaphylaxis | bush, more than 30 min from hospital | **Needs Rachel's review** — she decides, not the teacher. |
| Asthma | walking 2 km+, physical sport, swimming | Cleared, but the reliever must come. Parent confirms "reliever packed". If they don't: **cleared with conditions** and the teacher sees "check reliever at bus". |
| Type 1 diabetes | any trip over 4 hours or overnight | **Needs Rachel's review**, and a staff member trained in diabetes care must be on the trip. |
| Epilepsy | swimming or water activity | **Not cleared** unless the parent's note says the treating doctor has approved water activity *and* Rachel has reviewed. |
| Heart condition | physical sport, walking 2 km+ | **Needs Rachel's review**. |
| Mobility limitation | walking (any) | **Cleared with conditions**: the teacher records the arrangement (e.g. "wheelchair-accessible bus booked"). |
| Other | any | **Needs Rachel's review** if the parent ticks "this may affect the activities on this trip"; otherwise cleared. |

If a child has more than one condition, the strictest result wins: not cleared,
then needs review, then cleared with conditions, then cleared.

**Worked examples:**

1. Year 3 trip to the museum, walking under 2 km, food provided. Ava has
   anaphylaxis to peanuts, plan uploaded, auto-injector confirmed. Result:
   cleared with conditions (venue to be told about peanuts).
2. Same trip. Jonah has anaphylaxis to bees, plan uploaded, but the parent hasn't
   ticked the auto-injector. Result: not cleared, and the list says why.
3. Year 6 camp, overnight, swimming, bush. Lily has type 1 diabetes and asthma.
   Diabetes says review; asthma says cleared with conditions. Result: needs
   Rachel's review.
4. Year 5 swimming carnival. Sam has epilepsy, the parent's note says the doctor
   is happy. Result: still needs Rachel's review before Sam is cleared.

**Edge cases:**

- If a parent changes a medical note after the child has been cleared, the
  result is worked out again and the teacher in charge is told. A change can
  only make things stricter until someone looks at it; the teacher shouldn't find
  out on the bus.
- If the teacher adds an activity to a trip after consents are in (it happens:
  "oh, there's a pool"), every child's result is worked out again and parents of
  anyone affected are asked again.
- "Not cleared" doesn't mean "not going". I may clear a child after talking to
  the family. When I do, I write a reason, and the record keeps that it was my
  decision and why, separate from what the rules said.
- A trip can't be marked "ready to go" while anyone on it is "needs review" or has
  not answered. The teacher can take a child off the list instead.

Please don't let anyone except me change these rules in the app. If the table
needs to change, it comes to me.
