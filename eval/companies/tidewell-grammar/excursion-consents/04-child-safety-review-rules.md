exercises: a way-of-working change after an external review: a named approver for any change touching medical information, and a record of who viewed medical notes
---

We had our child-safety review last month and the reviewer asked two questions I
couldn't answer quickly: who has looked at a particular child's medical notes,
and who agreed to the last change in how they are shown.

From now on:

1. Every time a staff member opens a child's medical notes in this app, it
   should be recorded: who, when, which child, which trip. I need to be able to
   see that for one child, and for one staff member, without asking anyone.
2. Any change to this app that touches medical information — what is collected,
   who can see it, how long it's kept, what the rules do — needs my approval
   before it goes live. Anjali or Callum asking for it isn't enough. If I'm away,
   it waits. Changes that don't touch medical information (reminder emails,
   print layout) don't need me.
3. I'd like this to apply to any other app the school builds that holds medical
   information, not just this one.
