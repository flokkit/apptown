We still do excursion consent on paper. A note goes home, half of them come back
crumpled at the bottom of a bag, and on the morning of the trip a teacher is
standing at the bus ringing parents. I'd like this online.

What I need:

- A teacher sets up a trip: name, date, where, leaving and returning times,
  cost if any, which classes or year levels are going, the teacher in charge,
  and the date consent closes.
- Parents get a link and, for each child going, say yes or no, add or confirm
  medical notes (allergies, asthma, medication, anything else we should know),
  and give an emergency contact number for the day.
- The teacher in charge sees a list for the trip: who's cleared, who said no,
  who hasn't answered. They need to be able to print it for the bus.
- Medical notes should only be visible to the teachers going on that trip and
  to Rachel. Not the whole staff.

Staff sign in with their school accounts. Parents shouldn't need an account,
the link should be enough, but one parent mustn't be able to see another
family's children. Start with the junior school; the senior school can come later.
