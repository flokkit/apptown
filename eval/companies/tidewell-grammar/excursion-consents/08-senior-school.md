Senior school would like to start using the consents app from Term 1. Differences
from the junior school:

- Students in Years 7–12 have their own school email. Reminders can go to the
  student as well as the parent, but consent itself still has to come from a
  parent.
- Senior trips are often set up by the head of a faculty for several classes at
  once, and the teacher in charge can be different for each day of a
  multi-day trip.
- Year 11 and 12 students who are 18 can consent for themselves. We have maybe
  five a year. Their parents should not be emailed unless the student says so.

Junior school should work the same as now.
