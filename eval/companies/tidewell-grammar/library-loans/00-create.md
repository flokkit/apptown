The library system we've used for fifteen years is being switched off at the end
of the year (the company has stopped supporting it). I need something to replace
the parts we actually use:

- A catalogue: title, author, ISBN, barcode, which campus library it's in, shelf
  location (Dewey number or "fiction A–Z" or "picture books"), and whether it's
  available, on loan, lost or withdrawn.
- Loans: scan or type the student's or staff member's ID and the book's barcode,
  done. Returns the same. Due date set automatically.
- Each student's and staff member's current loans and history.
- Every night, work out what's overdue and send the notices.
- Students and parents can search the catalogue to see if a book is in. They
  don't need to sign in for that.

Two libraries, one at each campus, and books do move between them. Me and two
library assistants run it. I'll send the rules for loan periods and notices
separately because they're a bit involved.
