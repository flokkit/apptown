Here are the loan and overdue rules. I've tried to write down what we actually
do, including the bits that live in my head.

**Loan periods and limits**

| Borrower | Loan period | How many at once |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kindergarten – Year 2 | 1 week | 1 |
| Years 3–6 | 2 weeks | 3 |
| Years 7–10 | 3 weeks | 5 |
| Years 11–12 | 3 weeks | 8 |
| Staff | 6 weeks | no limit |

Reference books and anything marked "teacher resource" can't go out to students
at all. Staff can borrow them for 1 week.

A student at the limit can't borrow another until one comes back. The screen
should say why, not just beep.

**Holidays**

If a due date falls in the school holidays, it moves to the first school day of
the next term. Use the school's term dates, don't ask me to type them in; Callum
keeps them. Books borrowed in the last two weeks of Term 4 are all due on the
first day of Term 1 — the long holiday.

Nothing counts as overdue during the holidays, and no notices go out in the
holidays.

**Overdue notices**

- 1 day overdue: nothing. Kids forget, it's fine.
- 7 days overdue: first notice.
- 21 days overdue: second notice, copied to the class teacher (junior) or the
  homeroom teacher (senior).
- 42 days overdue: the book is marked "presumed lost" and the business office gets
  a list with the replacement cost. I'll look at it before they do anything.

Junior school (K–6) notices go to the parent, never to the student. Senior
school notices go to the student, and the 21-day notice goes to the parent too.

One notice per borrower per night, listing all their overdue books, not one email
per book.

**Worked examples** (say Term 2 starts Monday 27 April and Term 1 ended Friday 10
April):

1. Year 4 student borrows on Monday 30 March. Two weeks makes it Monday 13 April,
   in the holidays, so it's due Monday 27 April. First notice (if it's still out)
   on Monday 4 May, to the parent.
2. Year 9 student borrows on Thursday 2 April. Three weeks is Thursday 23 April,
   in the holidays, so due Monday 27 April.
3. Year 8 student, book due 1 June, still out. First notice 8 June to the student.
   Second notice 22 June to the student, the parent, and the homeroom teacher.
4. Staff member, book due 6 weeks after borrowing, overdue. Same 7-day and 21-day
   notices, but no "presumed lost" and no business office list — I'll chase staff
   myself.

**Edge cases**

- A student who leaves the school with books out: list them for me on the
  student's last day, don't wait for the notices.
- A book returned to the other campus counts as returned when it's scanned there.
- If a book is renewed, the overdue clock starts again from the new due date. A
  book can be renewed twice; after that it must come back to the library first.
- If a notice email bounces, I want to see that on the borrower's page.
