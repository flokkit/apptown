Some rules for the tour bookings, now that I've talked to the ambassadors:

- Tours leave every 20 minutes from 9:00 to 12:20 (so the last one ends at 12:50).
  Each tour takes up to 12 people. Two ambassadors per tour.
- Junior school tours (for families interested in K–6) start at Ridgeview and
  senior tours at Harbour Road; a family picks one.
- One booking per email address. Up to 6 people per booking.
- When a tour is full, families can join a waitlist for it and get an email if a
  place opens.
- Families should get a confirmation email with a link to cancel or change.
- Bookings close at 6pm the day before. Walk-ins on the day are handled at the
  front desk and don't need to be in the system.

Please don't ask for children's names on the booking. Year level of interest is
enough.
