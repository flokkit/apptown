AppTown

A software factory for the rest of us.

AppTown gives your team, or your clients, a ready-made place to build apps with AI coding agents. There's nothing to install on their side. Each app follows a way of working you choose, and they're all checked, deployed and backed up the same way, so looking after many apps doesn't mean looking after many different setups.

You run AppTown yourself, on your own computer or server. Take the tour.
