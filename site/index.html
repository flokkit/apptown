AppTown

AppTown gives your team a ready-made place to build small web apps with AI coding agents, in the browser or from a desktop agent via MCP. Each app follows a way of working you choose, with consistent checks and deployment. That makes a growing collection of apps easier to maintain and change.

You run AppTown yourself, on your own computer or server. Take the tour.

Why

AppTown started at home, building games with the kids. Agents could quickly bring ideas to life, but getting the games online was frustrating. "Why can't my friend see it?" "Now I want it to be multiplayer!" "What's Git?" There were deployment credentials, permissions, hosting and DNS to set up. Once the games were running, keeping them secure and up to date became another job.

Teams face the same problem with internal tools. An app might take an afternoon to build, but someone still has to look after it months later, often after the person who made it has moved on.

Shared conventions and a record of what changed help the next person understand the app and keep it useful.

Try it on your computer

On an Apple Silicon Mac with Docker Desktop, or Linux with Docker Engine:

curl -fsSL https://apptown.dev/try | sh

Run it on a server

On one Linux server with Docker (Compose 2.23 or later) and a domain name:

curl -fsSLO https://apptown.dev/compose.yaml
echo APPTOWN_DOMAIN=apptown.example.com > .env
docker compose up -d

First point apptown.example.com, *.preview.apptown.example.com and *.main.apptown.example.com at the server, and open ports 80 and 443. One server suits a small team that trusts everyone who writes projects. Running AppTown has the details.
