AppTown

AppTown gives your team, household or clients a ready-made place to build small web apps with AI coding agents, in the browser or from their own coding agent. Each app follows a way of working you choose and is checked and deployed the same way, so looking after many apps doesn't mean looking after many different setups.

You run AppTown yourself, on your own computer or server. Take the tour.

1.0 release candidate. The README has the trial and server install steps. Open source, Apache-2.0.

AppTown is made by Flokkit.
