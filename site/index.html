AppTown

A software factory for the rest of us.

AppTown helps small teams keep the apps they make with AI coding agents. You run it yourself, on your own machine or server.

Coding agents have dropped the cost of small custom apps to almost nothing, but owning an app that people can rely on is still a lot of work. AppTown gives a small team the habits of a careful engineering team: every change is checked, approved and recorded, and the apps keep running after the person who made them has moved on.
